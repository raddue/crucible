# Portable quality-gate verdict and model assurance

## Decision

Keep the quality-gate verdict about the artifact: PASS means its required reviews, fixes, and closure checks passed. It does **not** certify which model executed. Report execution assurance for every used model-bearing QG invocation whose output decides closure or mutates the accepted artifact: red-team, look-harder, fix agents, fix verifiers, stagnation/verdict judges, post-PASS Minor quick-fix, consumed review-mode consensus members and synthesis, and all consumed QG-internal siege attacker, synthesis, fix, and re-review invocations. A look-harder demotion does not erase the original review's contribution when it triggered that decision. Inventory every standalone `external_review` response actually consumed, including non-consensus responses appended to fixes and responses bridged into consensus; bridged responses are the same invocation and count once, and identity/source must be independently attested or that contribution is `unknown`. Model-authored parent QG/siege orchestration decisions or synthesis that select, demote, combine, or declare closure are critical invocations too; bind each to parent decision session/invocation evidence or move judgment into an attested child invocation. Deterministic routing, scoring, and checker steps are exempt unless they use a model. Define inventory as a recursive consumed-invocation graph: expand any consumed wrapper whose output derives from nested model calls, including siege Chain Analyst consensus into its consumed successful members and synthesis; an unbound node makes its parent result `unknown`. Deterministic mechanical checker roles are not model-bearing and remain subject to existing gate checks; include them if a model is used. Aggregate assurance is the weakest used invocation's state: `verified`, `configured`, or `unknown`; an invocation on an unapproved or below-high model is `unknown` for this high-tier assurance report, even if its identity is observed. Maintain a run-wide invocation inventory across rounds, chunks, look-harder, fixes/verifiers, judges, post-pass quick-fix, review-mode consensus, and QG-internal siege; each used invocation/member needs its own stable identity and consumption record. A siege result is not one invocation: missing child inventory or attestation makes that result `unknown`, even when its wrapper receipt is verified. A consensus result contributes each actually consumed responding member separately, not merely the common query; whenever synthesized output contributes findings, inventory and attest its separate synthesis invocation, including its request/response and required effort, otherwise downgrade that consensus contribution. A siege receipt used to clear the security gate is a used critical result even if it contributes no scored red-team findings. Compute terminal aggregate over all consumed critical invocations, not only terminal round. Preserve complete inventory through recovery; missing or incomplete inventory means `unknown`, never an assumed empty set. Failed or unused invocations do not contribute to aggregate; mandatory invocation failure still follows existing gate rules. No host, provider, Pi, 9router, TypeSafe Jev, or adapter is required to run the core gate.

A **high-assurance quality-gate PASS** is the conjunction `Verdict: PASS` and `ModelAssurance: verified` with every used critical reviewer mapped to an approved high-tier model. The assurance label never weakens review/receipt requirements. Plain PASS remains available with `configured` or `unknown`, but must be labeled plainly and must never be described as high-assurance. `high`/`xhigh` reasoning effort is configuration policy where supported, not a claim about hidden internal reasoning. Requested profiles and model-authored text never establish `verified`.

## Assurance contract

- `verified`: an allowlisted, versioned host adapter binds independent provider/endpoint-reported model identity to the **complete reviewer invocation**; an approved exact-ID mapping qualifies the model as high tier. The binding covers every model-bearing request/response used to produce the review, including tool-continuation turns, not just the final request. The adapter either verifies each request/response pair's model, route, and required `high`/`xhigh` effort, or enforces and attests a session invariant that prevents model/effort changes throughout the invocation. Host owns adapter capture and attestation/inventory writes outside reviewer-child write scope; evidence authenticates origin and binds to the dispatch/session, with permissions preventing child modification. Verify that origin and binding before `verified`; hashes/readback establish integrity only, not provenance. Child-writable or unverifiable evidence downgrades the invocation to `unknown`. Any unobserved, mixed, rewritten, or conflicting turn downgrades the invocation to `configured` or `unknown`. It attests accepted configuration, not unobservable internal behavior.
- `configured`: the requested exact model ID and effort meet an approved configuration policy, but actual execution lacks independent proof. A dispatch manifest field named `actual_model` is still only configured unless an adapter supplies the above binding. Unknown or unapproved aliases cannot become configured-high by name resemblance.
- `unknown`: missing or ambiguous configuration, mapping, provenance, or route evidence. Never infer a model from a child receipt, agent definition, provider family, or a previous run.

Store model mapping and adapter rules locally, versioned and approved per exact ID and route. New IDs and changed host/adapter/route fingerprints require review; no automatic family inheritance. A high-tier mapping change needs human approval and representative review-quality evidence. Each run's assurance is fresh and records a nonsecret evidence reference, adapter/version, qualifying model ID, policy version, and reason for downgrade. Keep credentials and raw transcripts outside markers and ledgers. Host adapters are optional extensions of this contract; unsupported hosts honestly report `configured` or `unknown`.

## Gate integration and compatibility

Add `ModelAssurance` and `ModelAssuranceInventory` (run ID, durable artifact reference, and SHA-256 digest) to the terminal marker; add `model_assurance` to the Tier-A ledger as an additive, optional display field. Add explicit marker finality `chunk-milestone|final-integration`; only `final-integration` can qualify for `HIGH-ASSURANCE QG PASS`. Retain MarkerVersion 2 and ledger schema_version 2: existing writers/readers use key-value and JSON objects, and `ledger_append.py` preserves extra keys. Update their documented field lists and all assurance-consuming parsers; do not alter existing required fields. A missing, malformed, partial, or digest-mismatched assurance record reads as `unknown`, never `verified`. Persist the bounded per-run inventory and its nonsecret evidence index outside disposable QG scratch (under `memory/quality-gate/model-assurance/<run-id>.json`); include stable invocation/member ID, parent/scope, consumed state, assurance result, and durable attestation reference for every consumed critical invocation. Keep `ArtifactHash` as the existing pre-gate input hash; add a distinct SHA-256 `FinalArtifactHash` for accepted output bytes, computed after all fixes including any post-PASS Minor quick-fix, to the inventory, terminal marker, and caller proof tuple. At opted-in action, callers compare `FinalArtifactHash` with the accepted artifact's current bytes; never use `ArtifactHash` for that comparison. For multi-file code gates, define accepted bytes as a canonical manifest of the exact gated path set: normalize repository-relative paths to `/` separators, reject absolute paths, `.`/`..` components and NUL, then sort by UTF-8 path bytes. Each entry has path, type (`regular`, `symlink`, or `deleted`), Git mode (`100644`, `100755`, `120000`, or `-` for a deletion tombstone), and lowercase SHA-256 hex digest: hash regular-file contents, hash symlink target bytes without following links, and use SHA-256 of empty bytes for deletion tombstones. Include untracked files only when listed in the gated set; reject missing or extra entries and special file types. Bind canonical real paths for `git common-dir` and worktree root. Encode domain/version tag, both identity paths, entry count, then each entry's path, type, mode, and digest in that fixed order; encode every field as UTF-8 bytes prefixed by unsigned 64-bit big-endian byte length. Hash the resulting bytes with SHA-256. Single-file documents may hash raw bytes. Freeze manifest after final QG fixes and post-PASS quick-fix, before marker/handoff; Warden/build compare it to the exact tree they authorize at their commit boundary, then revalidate against that committed tree before phase transition, push, or recovery action. Any gated-path/content mismatch invalidates QG assurance and blocks only opted-in action. Persist the expected chunk roster, each chunk's terminal status, and mandatory cross-chunk consensus membership and consumption in the durable hashed inventory before scratch cleanup; non-chunked runs declare an explicit single-artifact roster and applicable consensus set, including an explicit empty set. Callers read back and verify this roster against the final marker before selecting final-integration PASS. For `verified`, require a separate adapter/process-owned protected append-only consumption journal: before each model-bearing decision, atomically append each result's stable dispatch/member ID, round/chunk, result hash, parent/scope, consumed or unused disposition, and replay/rollback disposition before acting on it. A portable untrusted event log may record available ordinary evidence but cannot establish completeness or provenance; without protected adapter capture, assurance is only `configured` or `unknown` and ordinary artifact PASS continues. A failed protected write blocks `verified` and any opted-in action, never portable artifact closure. Store the protected journal and immutable dispatch/result records, including their hashes, in the same adapter/process-owned durable evidence area outside disposable scratch; retain them through marker lifetime and opted-in handoff. Inventory indexes these source records and hashes; do not duplicate them in another archive. Reconcile journal events to retained immutable dispatch/result records and their hashes, then reconcile the exact consumed-ID set to inventory; recovery replays this evidence before opted-in action. Missing or unavailable source records mean `unknown`; never derive completeness solely from inventory declarations. Record a look-harder trigger as consumed even when its original findings are later demoted/replaced. For consensus, record failed attempts and unused successful members as non-consumed dispositions, and only consumed successful members plus consumed synthesis as assurance members; reconcile every member against immutable consensus results. Missing, extra, duplicate, hash-mismatched, or unavailable records mean `unknown`. For each attestation reference, bind its content digest, protected origin, and dispatch/session binding in the inventory; evidence itself must remain durable or independently retrievable. On every opted-in next-phase transition, push handoff, or recovery-driven action, callers verify evidence accessibility, content digest, protected origin, and dispatch binding. Missing, changed, or unverifiable evidence downgrades assurance to `unknown` and blocks only that opted-in action; dangling references never remain `verified`. Apply fixed, documented entry-count and byte-size limits; overflow, missing attestation, failed write/readback, or incomplete membership downgrades assurance to `unknown`. Before writing a verified marker, read back the artifact, validate completeness and evidence references, and bind its run ID and digest in the marker. This artifact is the source of truth and must survive scratch cleanup; the optional Tier-A calibration-ledger emit remains separate and cannot be relied on for proof (it may be disabled, skipped, deduplicated, or size-limited). Existing terminal-round-only `model_resolution` retains its original shape/scope and unknown/indeterminate values where evidence is insufficient. Do not promote the current configured `actual_model` manifest field to `ran`. No changed `Verdict`, closure scoring, severity, or terminal vocabulary. Label quality-gate's own final result `PASS (QG model assurance: configured|unknown)` or `HIGH-ASSURANCE QG PASS` only when it is a complete final-integration marker and its full used critical-invocation set and durable inventory are verified. Per-chunk PASS markers are non-final milestones; their local assurance is non-qualifying and must never be selected or displayed as whole-gate high assurance. A final integration marker may be emitted only after every manifest-listed chunk has terminal status and all mandatory cross-chunk consensus is consumed. Callers selecting a PASS marker must verify final-integration status, complete chunk manifest/status set, and exact run/artifact binding, not merely select latest PASS by timestamp. The current consensus API does not expose stable per-member response identity/source; until it does, consensus members cannot be verified and QG assurance is at most `configured`/`unknown`. Warden's aggregate PASS covers additional temper, delve, and conditional siege/inquisitor legs: preserve its ordinary PASS label and display `QG-leg model assurance: <state>` separately. Whole-Warden opt-in is deferred: accept only `none|qg-verified` as caller policy until a distinct durable Warden proof object and expected-leg roster cover every used temper, delve, Warden-owned siege/inquisitor leg, bound to Warden run and QG proof, with readback on push and recovery. Warden cannot label whole run high-assurance from QG evidence alone. Build likewise records the quality-gate leg's assurance alongside its phase status, not an assurance claim about every build phase. No silent PASS-to-verified upgrade during recovery.

The existing Pi provenance checker still checks dispatch/receipt coverage. Its `actual_model` manifest comparison is configuration consistency only: absence downgrades assurance to `unknown`, not `GATE-NOT-RUN`; a mismatch with an explicitly mapped request remains a dispatch-consistency failure. Preserve required-leg and receipt-integrity failures as gate failures, but separate them from optional model evidence. Test a completed valid dispatch with no `actual_model` and an ordinary PASS plus `unknown` assurance. Missing optional assurance evidence alone does not block ordinary PASS. Callers that explicitly require high assurance block their own next action when assurance is insufficient; they do not rewrite a valid artifact verdict or pretend that the gate was skipped. This requirement is explicit caller policy, not the default for all Crucible users.

Persist caller opt-in with durable pipeline/run state as `RequiredModelAssurance: none|qg-verified` (or an equivalent explicitly scoped field). Build records it in its pipeline ledger; Warden records it in the run state/handoff that authorizes push. Before every next-phase transition, push handoff, and recovery-driven action, check the persisted requirement against the corresponding stored assurance and inventory; `qg-verified` requires verified final-integration QG evidence. `Status: PASS` remains artifact closure only and never satisfies this separate predicate. Whole-Warden opt-in (`warden-verified`) is unavailable until its distinct Warden-wide proof contract is defined and implemented; ordinary Warden PASS and optional QG-leg assurance remain unchanged. If encountered in persisted state, `warden-verified` blocks that opted-in action pending explicit user resolution; never treat it as `none`. If recovery finds a persisted requirement but missing/unknown assurance, or cannot persist the requested policy, block only that opted-in action and request fresh gating/user resolution; do not advance from PASS alone. Legacy state with no recorded opt-in retains ordinary behavior.

Build and warden persist `QGModelAssurance` and its proof tuple (`RunID`, pre-gate input `ArtifactHash`, accepted-output `FinalArtifactHash`, `ModelAssuranceInventory` path, inventory SHA-256 digest) alongside their existing `PASS` and opt-in state before deleting any temporary QG marker. On every next-phase transition, push handoff, and recovery-driven action, validate tuple against same QG run and retained inventory file, including file digest and inventory run/artifact binding; compare `FinalArtifactHash` to accepted artifact bytes and revalidate referenced attestation evidence and retained journal/source records. Missing or mismatched tuple, file, evidence, or source record becomes `unknown` and blocks only an opted-in action. An absent or invalid assurance becomes `unknown` on recovery. Their status display names it as the QG leg's assurance, while gate-ledger phase predicates continue to use `Status: PASS` for artifact closure plus the separate opt-in predicate above. A caller's opt-in requirement for whole-Warden assurance cannot be satisfied by the QG field alone. No pending-leg lifecycle, automatic retry loop, or global release block is needed solely for absent model telemetry. Existing gate recovery and marker rules remain. An adapter failure is a downgrade to `configured` or `unknown`, not permission to claim verified. If a reviewer result itself is missing, the ordinary gate fails independently of assurance status.

## First adapter and boundaries

Implement the portable core first. A later adapter may support Pi, Claude Code, or another host only after an independent observation can be bound to every model-bearing request/response used by the original critical reviewer, or after it enforces and attests an immutable model/effort session. A dispatch-level or final-response-only observation is insufficient for multi-turn review. For QG-internal siege, the adapter must verify the complete recursive consumed-invocation graph (attacker, synthesis, fix, re-review, nested Chain Analyst consensus members, and consumed synthesis), not only the parent result; failed or unused members are non-consumed, and any unbound consumed node downgrades the parent result. For review-mode consensus at any depth, require a stable consensus-call ID plus unique member ID, response ID, and source for each consumed successful member. Independently attest consumed standalone external-review response identity/source, whether consumed directly or bridged; count bridged responses once. Issue verification only for independently observed provider-transport responses; injected `additional_responses` remain unverified absent independent attestation. Persist only actual consumed membership, not attempted failures. Current consensus interface lacks those stable identifiers/source guarantees, so it cannot produce verified consensus evidence until extended. On this machine, Pi → 9router → OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions probes bound a run to its child session and provider response IDs, and revealed provider `gpt-6-sol` versus requested `cx/gpt-6-sol[1m]`. A `high` probe showed outgoing `high`, but gateway forwarding and route semantics were not proven. Those temporary logs are investigative, not gate evidence. 9router is neither a required dependency nor a generic proof source. A separate provider call cannot certify an earlier agentic review. Jev routing intent cannot certify actual execution.

## Verification and stop conditions

Tests cover ordinary PASS at `configured` and `unknown`, including no-adapter operation and failed protected-write opt-in blocking; every model-bearing closure/mutation invocation, including parent orchestration decisions, standalone non-consensus external review, bridged external responses counted once, fix/verifier/judge/post-pass quick-fix; mechanical checker separation; look-harder demotion consumption; recursive siege Chain Analyst consensus with missing/unbound consumed member; complete multi-turn binding and downgrade for mixed-model/mixed-effort or unobserved turns; canonical multi-file manifest ordering, file types, symlink/deletion/untracked policy, identity, and Warden/build commit-boundary revalidation; durable inventory and protected-journal write/readback, digest, run-ID, completeness, recovery after scratch deletion, reconciliation against immutable dispatch/result records, replay/rollback, partial consensus with failed and unused members, and unknown on missing or mismatched records; optional ledger disabled/oversized and inventory-limit overflow; consensus duplicate model IDs, distinct member/response IDs, synthesis invocation coverage, and injected `additional_responses`; forged child claims and child-writable evidence files, unauthenticated origin, wrong dispatch/response ID, unknown mapping, and downgrade on adapter error. Chunk milestones must never qualify as high-assurance; final marker selection must reject missing chunks, missing mandatory consensus, or wrong run/artifact binding. Recovery tests persist the QG proof tuple with `qg-verified` and prove every phase/push transition blocks on missing/mismatched run, artifact, inventory path, digest, or non-final marker, while retaining artifact `Status: PASS`. Whole-Warden opt-in remains unavailable; ordinary Warden PASS with unverified temper/delve/Warden siege/inquisitor remains ordinary PASS. Existing `scripts/run_tests.sh` and relevant behavioral evals must pass. Independent quality gate must challenge both false verified claims and accidental blocking of portable ordinary PASS.

Stop high-assurance certification for any route whose metadata is child-writable, whose response model merely echoes the requested selector, whose request/response cannot bind to the reviewer dispatch, or whose required effort may be silently rewritten. Do **not** stop the core quality gate on those grounds. This replaces the earlier universal pending/PASS-blocking design by explicit user decision; #747 severity nomenclature remains separate.
